# Gap scout: fuel for power as its own mechanism (30 Oct 2026)

Question: is there a distinct, falsifiable mechanism joining the gas fuel story (Permian, pipes, LNG; now in building-your-own-power) and the nuclear fuel story (SWU, HALEU; now in nuclear-for-ai), strong enough for a new deep piece and a "Fuel for power" map layer? Hypothesis tested: fuel is contracted and built on a different, later clock than the plant, so fuel becomes the binding step for some routes.

## Verdict: UPDATE (two pieces), not a new deep piece

The hypothesis holds for one route only (HALEU reactors), and nuclear-for-ai already says so in full. For gas, the primary sources point the other way: new pipe is contracted by the same buyers, at about the same time, and lands on about the same date as the turbines (2027 to 2030 against turbine slots sold to 2030 and 2032). Fuel runs in parallel with the plant, not behind it. A joined piece would recombine two existing mechanisms under one name, which fails quality test 1. What is genuinely new is two pieces of evidence, each better as an update:

1. building-your-own-power, "The fuel" section: the pipe has its own contract clock (open season, precedent agreements, FERC certificate, in-service), about four years, run by the gas buyer; the newest Permian pipe is contracted for a hyperscaler's generation and lands in Q3 2030, level with turbine slots. Plus the pipeline company as power seller (Williams Socrates), which bears on the open second limb of the 22 Sep claim.
2. nuclear-for-ai, "The fuel" section: the running fleet's uranium is not yet contracted for most of the next decade (EIA unfilled requirements), and DOE's HALEU allocations have gone to eight then ten selections with no stated tonnage per company on the department's page.

## Why not a new deep piece, against the three tests

1. Mechanism the site lacks: no. "New nuclear waits on HALEU" is nuclear-for-ai's thesis. "Gas output is set by oil drilling and limited by pipe" is building-your-own-power's fuel section. The tested joining claim (fuel lags the plant) fails for gas on primary dates: SSE November 2027, Delta Access Q1 2029, Permian Link Q3 2030, Southeast Connector Q4 2030, against turbine slots sold to 2030 and 2032.
2. Primary sources: yes for the updates above.
3. Beyond the headline: yes, modestly. A PM learns that pipe for a hyperscaler's own generation is contracted take-or-pay and lands in 2030, the same year as a heavy turbine slot; that the fastest own-generation route on record was built by the pipeline owner; and that most of the fleet's 2026 to 2035 uranium is still uncontracted.
