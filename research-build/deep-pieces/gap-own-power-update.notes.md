### 2. EIA-860 instructions: who must report. PRIMARY

URL: https://www.eia.gov/survey/form/eia_860/instructions.pdf (OMB 1905-0129, expires 07/31/2029; downloaded 2 Nov 2026)

Quote: existing plants respond if "The plant's total generator nameplate capacity is 1 Megawatt (MW) or greater and The plant's generator(s), or the facility in which the generator(s) resides, are connected to the local or regional electric power grid and have the ability to draw power from or deliver power to the grid". Proposed plants likewise, and non-coal, non-nuclear proposed plants only if "expected to begin commercial operation within 5 years".

Use: an off-grid plant like Socrates is outside the federal generator inventory, so EIA cannot count off-grid power against grid connections. This explains why the 22 Sep claim's second limb (own generation against comparable grid connections) and its watch item ("Behind-the-meter capacity actually energised against grid-connected") have no primary head-to-head. Behind-the-meter plants at a grid-connected site are inside scope; fully islanded ones are not. Worth one sentence in "Where later evidence bears on it".

Form EIA-860M instructions (https://www.eia.gov/survey/form/eia_860m/instructions.pdf): monthly reporting begins "when either a new generator is within 12 months of entering commercial operation".
