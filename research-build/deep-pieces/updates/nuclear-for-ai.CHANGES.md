## LinkedIn draft (optional; the update is modest)

At the end of 2025, contracts covered at most 174 million of the 360 million pounds of uranium America's reactor owners expect to need over the next ten years.

For 2027 almost everything is bought. For 2030, 40 per cent is still unsigned. For 2033, 91 per cent.

That is how utilities buy, in steps and years ahead, so it is not a shortage. But it sits beside two dates: Russian enrichment waivers end with 2027, and America's only HALEU cascade has a lease that ends on 1 October 2027, with no further government production orders planned.

The old reactors still come first for AI. Their fuel has its own clock too.
