### What the contents say (only where a primary source could be read)

**ISO New England.** ISO Newswire, "ISO-NE files proposal with FERC to address 'large loads'", 13 Nov 2026, https://isonewswire.com/2026/11/13/iso-ne-files-proposal-with-ferc-to-address-large-loads/ ; filing package https://www.iso-ne.com/static-assets/documents/100041/nov_13_large_loads_filing.zip (not opened); proposal summary (9 Oct 2026) https://www.iso-ne.com/static-assets/documents/100040/large_loads_integration_proposal_information_summary_10.09.26.pdf (read).
- Joint filing by ISO-NE, NEPOOL and the transmission owners' committee. Study process "will be implemented on June 1, 2027".
- Large Load: "demand of 50 MW or greater"; existing loads become Large Loads on a 50 MW or more increase over their demand as of 18 June 2026.
- Three services: Firm Contract Demand Service (needs network upgrades and, under "bring your own new capacity", matching new capacity); Interim Contract Demand Service, "a temporary, non-firm transmission service provided before network upgrades are complete"; Non-Firm Contract Demand Service, "a permanent, non-firm transmission service".
- Cut rule: "a Non-Firm Large Load Re-Dispatch Price of $250/MWh". Newswire: the region reaches that price in about 1% of hours. Non-firm and interim service are left out of the installed capacity requirement. Bring-your-own-capacity detail continues in stakeholder talks in 2027.
- Touches: the tiers table row "ISO New England | To be filed by 16 November | Not filed | None" (now filed 13 Nov; a permanent non-firm tier plus an interim one, cut by price at $250/MWh; no customer yet). Also the sources line ("PJM and PJM transmission owner filings due 16 November 2026"). ISO-NE becomes a third grid with a filed cut-first tier beside SPP and PJM, which bears on the claim's "at least two of its seven organised grids" (it does not yet count: not in force, no data centre on it).

**PJM (ER27-531, the large-load compliance filing).** PJM Inside Lines, "PJM Plan Would Cut Interconnection Process Time in Half", 16 Nov 2026, https://insidelines.pjm.com/pjm-proposal-would-cut-interconnection-process-time-in-half/
- Named "Fast Energization": studies new generation and transmission service for large loads together, to cut study time "in half", from about two years to one.
- Earlier, stricter readiness and financial requirements; a quick first screen; states get upgrade cost information for large loads "so they can decide who pays".
- Includes transmission services for large loads that limit their use of the system, "including an interim service while Network Upgrades are built". Keeps "first-ready, first-served".
- PJM asked FERC for a 60-day comment period and an order within 120 days.
- Touches: line 48 ("PJM's answer, to be filed on 16 November, is one 12-month process a year for power stations and large loads alike"): filed; "about two years to one" fits, but the release does not say "one cycle a year", so that wording needs the transmittal. Line 77 ("PJM and its utilities file their answers by 16 November 2026"): now filed. Table row PJM ("for all large loads, to be filed 16 November 2026"): now filed 16 Nov, ER27-531, proposed effective 16 Mar 2027.
- NOT READ: whether the interim service is the Interim NITS the piece names, its cut terms, and the TOs' ER27-520 contents.

## 2. PJM ride-through filing: LANDED

FERC eLibrary: PJM, "Operational Requirements for Large Computational Load and Annual Reporting", ER27-522-000, accession 20261116-5309, filed 16 Nov 2026, proposed effective 16 Jan 2027 (with declarations of Egan and Choi). PJM Inside Lines, "PJM Proposes New Rules to Protect Grid Reliability as Data Center Demand Grows", 16 Nov 2026, https://insidelines.pjm.com/pjm-proposes-new-rules-to-protect-grid-reliability-as-data-center-demand-grows/
- New Large Computational Load category, 50 MW or greater; voltage and frequency ride-through "effective January 16, 2027".
- New LCLs must show compliance as a condition of interconnection and operation; existing LCLs within one year of the effective date or of commercial operation, whichever is later.
- On 22 July "nearly 3.9 GW" of data centres moved to onsite generation after a routine disturbance, "the largest such load transfer in PJM's history".
- PJM serves about 10 GW of existing LCL and forecasts additions of up to 30 GW by 2030 (new figure; not in the piece).
- Ramp-rate and enhanced ride-through: further stakeholder work, no date in the release.
- Touches: line 169 ("PJM files its own on 16 November") and the fold's "FERC filing 16 November 2026": now filed, ER27-522. The 16 Jan 2027 and existing-site dates match the piece's fold. The voltage and frequency bands in the fold come from PJM's committee decks; I could not check them against the filed tariff (not opened). Note the docket's title adds "Annual Reporting", which the piece does not mention. 3.9 GW matches the piece.
