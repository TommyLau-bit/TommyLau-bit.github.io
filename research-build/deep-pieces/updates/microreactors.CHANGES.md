- **NRC rule sentence: FIXED.** Source: 91 FR 67195 (26 Oct 2026, doc 2026-21801, effective 25 Nov 2026), govinfo full text. The rule does not let an applicant cite a bare "earlier DOE or military authorisation as evidence of safety". New 50.43(f) and revised 53.440(a)(1) let an application "include consideration of relevant information gathered from a utilization facility that has been previously authorized by the Department of Energy or the Department of Defense and that has been tested and has demonstrated the ability to function safely", and the applicant must show how the authorisation satisfies NRC regulations; the NRC still reviews independently. Covers Part 50/52 and Part 53 applicants; Part 57 (microreactors) only in the proposed rule. Sentence now: "a commercial licence applicant may cite safety evidence from a reactor the DOE or military authorised and tested."
