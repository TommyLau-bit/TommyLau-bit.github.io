## Draft LinkedIn post (only if Tommy rates this update substantial)

1.5 gigawatts. That is the on-site power Tenaga's own generating arm agreed in August to study for one DayOne campus in Selangor.

Malaysia now gives data centres grid power only from the spare margin. Those it will not serve are sent to build their own. In Parliament on 28 October, the government said self-generation means solar for their own use, with nothing exported to the grid. Backup generators must also be ready to cut load when reserves run low.

Singapore allows on-site power too, but it buys nothing there. The new licence caps the computing load a hall may run, not where its electricity comes from.

Own power gets round a queue. It does not get round a ration.
