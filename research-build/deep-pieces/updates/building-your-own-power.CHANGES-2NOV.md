## Draft LinkedIn post (house style of brand/linkedin-posts.md)

In a year, planned US gas power grew from 40.5 GW to 71.1 GW. The part actually being built grew from 9.6 GW to 13.1 GW.

That is my sum from EIA's monthly generator inventory, and it is the gap behind every campus that plans to build its own power. The plans arrive faster than the machines. A second heavy-turbine maker now quotes deliveries around 2030 to 2031, and an engine maker says its lead times keep rising.

The update also covers why federal data cannot see off-grid plants at all, and a gap between Homer City's stated target and its EIA entry.
