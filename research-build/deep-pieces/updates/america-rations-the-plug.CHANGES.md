## Fact-check (10 Nov)

Sources reopened: FERC 196 FERC ¶ 61,245, ER26-3380-000 / EL26-108-000 (29 Sep 2026), full 97-page PDF from pjm.com (pp. 1, 3, 9, 62 to 65, 83); PJM Inside Lines, "PJM Files Amended Reliability Backstop Proposal" (29 Oct 2026).

- Answer line "FERC can send the bill to a zone, the area one utility serves; only the states can send it to a customer": PASS. Order p. 63 accepts initial assignment to Electric Distributor zone/areas "with the ultimate cost allocation to the LSEs as determined by the state and/or RERRA"; p. 65 quotes "states determine how Commission-approved rates are collected among the relevant retail consumers". Fair plain-English reading (strictly PJM proposes and FERC approves; RERRAs as well as states).
- "about 6,800 MW", "one-time purchase of new capacity": PASS (order p. 1; 6,831 MW initial target; PJM release "approximately 6,800 MW").
- "FERC suspended it to 28 February 2027, because PJM's measure of load growth could miss some new large loads": FIXED. The order (p. 9) suspends for five months and sets a paper hearing on three issues (cost allocation, transmission owner exit rules, LSE collateral), not the load measure alone. The load-measure finding (pp. 63 to 64) is the core of the cost-allocation issue. Now "suspended it to 28 February 2027, mainly over the bill: PJM's measure of load growth could miss some new large loads." Evidence fold now names the three issues.
- Refiling terms (actual 2026 summer peak to 2028/29 forecast, zone by contribution, up to 15 years, MW-weighted average maximum $555/MW-day, 1 Mar / 28 Mar / 10 May, target recalculated and reduced by bilateral contracts): PASS against the PJM release. "each zone now pays" changed to "each zone would pay" (proposal, not in force). "at a weighted average of at most $555" shortened to "averaging at most $555" to stay under 3,600 words; the exact MW-weighted wording stays in the fold.
- "Which customers in a zone pay, FERC wrote, is for the states": PASS (pp. 63, 65).
- Governors: FIXED. The quoted fragment is verbatim (p. 65), but the pledge (issued with the White House National Energy Dominance Council) is to "use all available authorities to ensure that their state public utility commissions design rate class structures" so LSEs allocate the cost; "pledged to load that cost on" overstated a direct act. Now "pledged to steer that cost to". Fold now says the White House and governors agreed to "use all available authorities".
- "A site escapes the charge by bringing its own power": FIXED to "would escape the charge by buying its own capacity". The principle says "procured capacity", which can be bought, not only built; and it is a pledge, not in force.
