# What the Luminous prep teaches, translated for the journal (Tommy, 6 Nov 2026)

Tommy's direction: do not copy the Luminous slides (the stubborn machine, the two legs and so on) into every piece. Study why they were built that way for that prep, then translate the reasons into each article, only where its own topic needs them. Forcing a pattern onto a piece that does not need it is a failure.

Sources studied: ~/Desktop/jobs/archive/Luminous/Luminous_Anchor_and_Developments.pptx (25 slides) and AI_Infrastructure_Stack_Learning_Deck.pptx (30 slides).

## Why Luminous was built the way it was
The prep served one purpose. A reader with ten years of follow-up questions (a PM) had to see the mechanism from the inside, so that the view held up one level below the headline. The slides held the facts; the view had to be the reader's own. Every device served that purpose:

1. **Clear the word before the argument.** "Power is three problems sharing one word" came first because the argument fails if the listener hears the wrong "power". Translation: before arguing, settle the one word the topic's confusion lives in. Only that word, only once.
2. **The argument is a chain, in order, each step forcing the next.** The five steps were the thesis itself, not a summary of it. Translation: the reader should be able to retell the claim as a sequence of causes. If a step does not force the next, the chain is wrong, not the wording.
3. **Show the inside of the binding step.** The "stubborn machine" slide existed because for the interconnection thesis, the reason the choke point is slow is physical, and seeing that is what made it click. Translation: at the choke point, show what physically or institutionally makes it slow, in the form the topic needs (a process, a number traced, a rule). Six cards is the Luminous form, not the principle.
4. **Name why the market misses it, when that is the edge.** "Months vs years: an asymmetry of attention, not information" was the investable point of that thesis. Translation: say plainly what people count or watch instead, only if the piece's claim is a mispricing of attention. Do not invent one.
5. **Decode the one number a sceptic will probe.** "The number to own" took the most probed figure one level below: what it is measured against, how it is built, the trap. Translation: every piece has one or two numbers its claim rests on; show how each is built and what it is measured against, in the text, before a sceptic asks.
6. **Analogies from things the reader owns, mapped part by part.** Refinery, pipeline, stove; a city of restaurants needing gas hookups. Translation: one analogy, every part named, and the place it breaks known to the writer.
7. **Volunteer the falsifiers, tied to the part of the claim each one attacks.** Luminous split the thesis into two legs because that thesis had two separable legs (a policy one and a physics one). Translation: say what would prove the piece wrong; where the claim has distinct parts that different evidence attacks, say which test hits which part. Where it is one claim, keep it one.
8. **Developments are positions: concede first, then push back.** Translation: when a new development cuts against the piece, state it fairly, give ground where it is due, then say precisely why the claim still stands or what would change it.
9. **One idea seen at more than one scale, when it truly holds at each.** "One idea, three altitudes" worked because rack, grid and load really did share one constraint. Translation: use it only where the piece's idea genuinely repeats at another scale and adds something. Never as a recap.
10. **Plain, concrete, confident, no fluff.** Second person for the reader's experience; short sentences; numbers only when they carry the point.

## How to apply it
For each piece, diagnose which of these the piece already does well and which it fails, then fix only the failures, inside the current length limits, cutting repetition to make room. A piece that already does a thing well is left alone. The test is whether a PM reading it could retell the chain, see why the choke point binds, and defend the key number one level down.
