# Fact-check: nuclear-for-ai-depth (7 November 2026)

Adversarial check of `src/content/journal/nuclear-for-ai-depth.md` (source `nuclear.src.md`, figures in `assemble.py`, `charts.py`, `illos.py`), the claim `nuclear-order-of-holdings` and the three "Where later evidence bears on it" notes.

Opened this session and confirmed directly: the EIA Electric Power Monthly Table 6.07.B (nuclear 91.1, wind 33.8, solar 24.0 in 2025; 90.8 and 93.0 for nuclear in 2024 and 2023; released 23 Oct 2026); the EIA 2025 UMAR Table 16 (Russia 3,284 of 12,710 thousand SWU; US 2,930; foreign 9,780); and the FERC order 195 FERC ¶ 61,162 text (issued 1 June 2026; "may not be able to obtain full deliverability until at least December 31, 2030"; "potentially ... enable Crane to be fully operational before December 31, 2030"; 760 MW of CIRs; Crane "859 MW"; "invested about $1.6 billion"; transfer "may not be one-to-one"; resubmitting would delay an agreement "at least to 2028"). Also checked: Vistra's Q4 2025 call (26 Feb 2026), DOE's first HALEU allocation round (9 Apr 2025) and Meta and TerraPower (Jan 2026). For everything else I relied on the primaries cited in the three research files. Where the only source is press or a search excerpt, the row is marked UNVERIFIED.

Arithmetic, all recomputed: 785,304 / 4,425,029 = 17.7%. 3,284 / 12,710 = 25.8%. 2,930 / 12,710 = 23.1%. 1.6bn / 835 MW = 1,916. 4.3bn / 890 = 4,831. 0.8bn / 158 = 5,063. 31bn / 2,234 = 13,876. C$7.7bn / 300 = 25,667. 1,900 kg UF6 x 0.676 = 1.28 tU. The binding sum is 835 + 1,920 + 1,121 + 615 + 690 + 890 + 2,609 = 8,680. The uprate sum is 890 + 190 + 30 + 433 + 96 = 1,639. Restarts 835 + 800 + 615 = 2,250. HALEU 75 + 345 + 50 = 470. SWU (feed 0.711%, tails 0.25%): 19.75% needs 41.0 SWU/kg; 5% needs 7.9; 4.5% needs 6.9. Every sum and division is correct as computed. The problems are in what the sums include and what they are said to show.
