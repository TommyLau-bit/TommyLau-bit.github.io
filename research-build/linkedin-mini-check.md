## Reasons for each FIX (depth piece or fact-check)

- **M01:** the 60% is maximum demand connected in Tenaga's system. The piece keeps "booked" for supply contracts (Johor FACTCHECK #5 and #7).
- **M04:** the Power segment is gas, nuclear, hydro and steam (own-power FACTCHECK item 25).
- **M05:** Constellation said delivery "could be at risk" until the end of 2030, and FERC said the transfer could "potentially" help. The piece says what it buys is less certain than the headlines (nuclear FACTCHECK #15). "Hundreds of miles" is not in the piece.
- **M07:** the piece gives the GB200 NVL72 at about 120 kW on Nvidia's user guide, about eighty hairdryers, wardrobe-sized. Jane Street's tour and the 10 to 40 kW range were cut. Pontecorvo recalls 10 to 15 kW as dense (heat FACTCHECK E3 and E4).
- **M13:** the piece credits 1,400 W/m² to Musk. SpaceX's own page implies about 1,090 W/m², near 47°C.
- **M19:** the piece says targets "get vague" beyond 200 kW. Kyber was shown again in March 2026 with no power figure or date. The 576 GPUs and "by 2027" no longer stand.
- **M29:** the piece now carries Q3 at 29.8% against 30.3%. "Two points year on year" for Q2 is not confirmed, so it was dropped.
- **M31:** the reactor is designed by GE Vernova and Hitachi. OPG targets grid connection by the end of 2030. The restarts are due 2027 to early 2029, so "the same years" no longer holds (nuclear FACTCHECK #33).
- **M32:** on the same wire size the piece uses Nvidia's October 2025 figure of 157% more. The May 2025 85%/45% was on a different basis (rack FACTCHECK E1).
- **M33:** the sum is 9.8, not 9.7. The dry cooler is backed by a chiller, and size explains part of the gap (heat FACTCHECK E9, F20).
- **M37:** the piece and Johor FACTCHECK #80 say "about two chip generations behind". "Three flagships" counts Blackwell Ultra as a generation, so this is a judgement call.
- **M39:** Starcloud-1, 60 kg with one H100, flew in November 2025 and is charted in the piece.
- **M41:** plug FACTCHECK #18 could not find "available for revenue generation". The piece quotes "power being consumed by IT equipment" and says every tranche was delivered.
- **M44:** no larger-reactor application has been filed, so "not ruled on" implies a pending case (nuclear FACTCHECK #27).
- **M45:** the piece says the SST "toward 2029". Nvidia groups the hall-level options under near-term readiness (rack FACTCHECK F1).
- **M51:** the rule is still a proposal. Existing sites have until the later of 16 January 2028 or a year after switch-on.
- **M56:** the lease is an initial four years. Power comes around 2034 (piece and chart). The 16-year residual value guarantee now features (own-power FACTCHECK item 4). "Owed whether or not AI demand holds up" is not in the piece.
- **M57:** the piece now reads "a squeeze on price and timing, not a wall". The 118m lb cushion overstated, so the piece splits out the enriched and fabricated stock (nuclear FACTCHECK #19).
- **M58:** the piece says the finance chief credited volume, leverage and productivity, "not richer content".
- **From lines:** hong-kong-has-the-plug titles are retired (M11, M24, M37, M63). The old nuclear title changes (M05, M18, M31, M44, M57).
