# Microreactors: the reactors, the buyers, the economics, the licensing

Research slice for the microreactors piece. Checked 7 Nov 2026. Primary sources first; trade press marked [TRADE, lead only]. Pages that refused direct fetch (403) are marked [not opened]; their wording comes from search excerpts.

## 1. Definitions

- **INL:** "factory produced, transportable, and self-regulating", "power outputs in the 1–20 MWe range typically". INL/RPT-24-80433, Aug 2024, abstract. https://gain.inl.gov/content/uploads/4/2025/03/2024-Document-INL-RPT-24-80433.pdf
- **DOE says thermal:** "1-20 megawatts of thermal energy"; "up to 10 years without refueling". DOE NE, 26 Feb 2021. https://www.energy.gov/ne/articles/what-nuclear-microreactor
- **IAEA:** SMRs are "up to 300 MW(e) per unit"; microreactors are a subset, "typically up to 10 MW(e)". IAEA, 2023 (archived copy; live page not opened). https://www.iaea.org/newscenter/news/what-are-small-modular-reactors-smrs
- **NRC, proposed Part 57:** no power limit; entry by a 1 rem (10 mSv) accident-dose criterion and "not to exceed 10 metric tons" of heavy metal. 91 FR, 1 May 2026. https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-05-01/pdf/2026-08550.pdf
- **MWe vs MWt:** heat vs electricity. BANR 75 MWt for 20 or 22 MWe (27 to 29%); eVinci 15/5 (33%); Aalo-X 30/10 (33%); INL LMTR-20 20/7.24 (36%).

## 3. DOE Reactor Pilot Program and the 2026 criticalities

- **The programme:** 12 Aug 2025, 11 projects, 10 companies: Aalo, Antares, Atomic Alchemy, Deep Fission, Last Energy, Oklo, Natura, Radiant, Terrestrial, Valar. Goal: at least three critical by 4 July 2026. https://www.energy.gov/ne/us-department-energy-reactor-pilot-program ; DOE fact sheet, 25 Jul 2026, https://www.energy.gov/articles/fact-sheet-golden-era-american-nuclear-energy-has-arrived
- **Deadline** from EO 14301 (ANS, 2 Jul 2026, https://www.ans.org/news/article-8178/). DOE, 6 Jul 2026, https://www.energy.gov/articles/department-energy-celebrates-fourth-criticality-ahead-july-4th-goal

**Criticalities in 2026 under DOE authorisation:**

1. **Antares Mark-0:** 4 Jun 2026, INL. A "zero-power fueled criticality demonstration" (DOE fact sheet).
2. **Valar Ward 250:** 18 Jun 2026, Utah San Rafael Energy Lab, the first DOE-authorised reactor outside a national laboratory. It reached 10 kWt on 22 Jun during power ascension (ANS, 2 Jul 2026 [TRADE]).
3. **Deployable Energy Unity:** late 30 Jun 2026, INL, zero power. It came through the **Nuclear Energy Launch Pad** (NRIC), not the Pilot Program.
4. **Aalo-X:** 00:20 on 4 Jul 2026, INL, zero power (DOE, 6 Jul 2026; Aalo release, 6 Jul 2026, https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260706396342/en).
5. **Oklo Groves Isotope Test Reactor:** evening of 5 Aug 2026, Lockhart, Texas, "low power", the first on private land. DOE, 6 Aug 2026, https://www.energy.gov/ne/articles/office-nuclear-energy-celebrates-fifth-advanced-reactor-criticality (says "last night"). A 100 W maximum thermal rating appears in search summaries only, not in Oklo's own release.
- **Also critical, at NNSA's NCERC:** eVinci, 24 Aug 2026 (zero power); LANL's ZiaCore, Apr–May 2026 [TRADE].
- **Not yet critical:** Radiant Kaleidos (DSA approved 29 Oct 2026, readiness review still to come; it missed the July target). Last Energy's RELLIS pilot (DSA approved 7 Oct 2026; no criticality reported by 7 Nov). ANS, 2 Nov 2026.
- **No electricity sold** to any paying customer by any US microreactor, as far as I could find.

## 4. Military buyers

- **Army Janus:** launched 14 Oct 2025 (army.mil/article/288905); awards 26 Aug 2026 (https://www.army.mil/article/294891/). Up to **$2.2bn**, FY2027–FY2031, DIU Other Transaction agreements, milestone payments, contractor-owned and operated. Antares (Fort Bragg), BWXT (Fort Campbell), GA-EMS (Fort Hood), Radiant (Fort Benning), Westinghouse (Fort Drum). 20+ units expected; first, "regulated by the Army", by **September 2028**. No size range, no split. Radiant's share of up to $750m for up to 15 units: WNN, 27 Aug 2026 [TRADE].
- **EO 14299** (23 May 2025; 90 FR 22581, https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2025-05-29/html/2025-09796.htm): Section 3 requires an Army-regulated reactor operating at a domestic installation "no later than September 30, 2028". Section 1 asks for power that "cannot be disrupted by external threats or grid failures", including for AI computing at installations. Section 5(b) creates a HALEU fuel bank of at least 20 t.
- **Air Force ANPI:** pairings were announced in Apr 2026. Radiant goes to Buckley SFB, Westinghouse to Malmstrom AFB and Antares to JBSA. Goal: one reactor "by 2030 or sooner" (POWER's headline says 2028). WNN, 24 Apr 2026, https://www.world-nuclear-news.org/articles/us-air-force-announces-selections-for-microreactor-deployments ; af.mil release https://www.af.mil/News/Article-Display/Article/4465439/ [not opened].
- **Eielson AFB (Oklo):** DLA Notice of Intent to Award, 2025, for a 30-year firm-fixed-price contract after an NRC licence; "at least 5 MWe" (Oklo 10-Q Q2 2026); NEPA under way Sep 2026, completion est. 2030. No final contract. https://www.eielson.af.mil/microreactor/ [not opened]
- **Pele:** "power military equipment in 2028" (BWXT page).
- **Why the military buys, in its own words.** The primary texts talk about grid risk and resilience, not diesel:
  - Army, 14 Oct 2025: "vulnerability to grid instability, natural disasters, or adversarial attacks".
  - Army, 3 Dec 2025: "assured, carbon-free power for remote locations, contested logistics environments".
  - Air Force (Borders): missions "never be held at risk by a power outage".
  - No Army release I opened mentions diesel.

## 5. Economics

- **INL/RPT-24-80433 (Aug 2024), reference LMTR-20 (20 MWt, 7.24 MWe):**
  - First of a kind: overnight cost ~$14,600/kWe excluding first fuel ($19,761/kW including it), LCOE **$325/MWh**.
  - NOAK: ~$6,000/kWe, LCOE **$119/MWh** (abstract "~$120"; conclusion "~$150/MWh range"; $146 in one case).
- **INL/EXT-21-63214** (Shropshire, Black and Araújo, *Global Market Analysis of Microreactors*, Jun 2021, §2.4, p. 8), https://inldigitallibrary.inl.gov/sites/sti/sti/Sort_47218.pdf :
  - "remote locations... between $0.15/kWh and $0.60/kWh, where electricity is typically produced by diesel generators", in a paragraph sourced to **NEI 2019a**, not UAA. NEI microreactor cost: $0.14–0.41/kWh FOAK, $0.09–0.33 at 50 units.
  - **UAA 2020a** (p. 10): small rural Alaska $0.35–0.60/kWh (avg $0.52); hubs $0.17–0.48; Railbelt $0.20–0.28.
- **EIA prices, 2025 annual average (EPM Feb 2026, preliminary):** industrial **8.62 c/kWh**, commercial 13.41, residential 17.30, all sectors 13.63. https://www.eia.gov/energyexplained/electricity/prices-and-factors-affecting-prices.php
- **Electricity sold:** none found. Next to make power: Antares Mark-1 (2027), Kaleidos at DOME; both tests, not sales.

## 7. Licensing and transport

- **Part 53 is final:** dated 30 Mar 2026, effective 29 Apr 2026, optional. https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/03/30/2026-06048/ [not opened].
- **Part 57 (microreactors) is proposed:**
  - 1 May 2026; comments closed 15 Jun 2026. No traditional EPZ; factory fuelling and testing under manufacturing licences; remote operation contemplated; a fuelled reactor may be its own Part 71 package.
  - The NRC says the process "should enable the NRC to issue an OL within 6–12 months after accepting an application", if the application is complete.
  - **No final rule found.**
- **Part 71 proposed rule** ("Modernizing Package Certification Requirements", NRC-2025-1667, 27 Jul 2026): an optional, higher §71.47 radiation limit for Type B packages "including transportable microreactors", plus an equivalent-safety pathway. Not final. https://public-inspection.federalregister.gov/2026-15117.pdf
- **EO 14300** (23 May 2025): the NRC must reach a final decision on any new reactor application within 18 months; final rules are due within 18 months (NRC date: **23 Nov 2026**). The NRC chair has conceded some final rules will miss it (ANS, 13 Mar 2026 [TRADE]).
- **Commission papers:** SRM-SECY-24-0008, 17 Jun 2025 (factory fuel loading if criticality precluded; factory testing). SECY-25-0052 (Nth-of-a-kind), SRM 13 Nov 2025, Option 2 (ML25317A644). List: https://www.nrc.gov/reactors/new-reactors/advanced/modernizing/microreactors/reg-activities.html (updated 14 Sep 2026).
- **ADVANCE Act §208:** risk-informed microreactor strategies in eight areas (staffing, oversight, security, siting, emergency preparedness and others); guidance due **9 Jan 2026**, implementation **9 Jul 2027**; still "under way" on the NRC page.
- **How long licensing takes:**
  - No microreactor holds an NRC operating licence.
  - KRONOS: CP application 31 Mar 2026, docketed 18 May 2026, draft safety evaluation Feb 2027 (NRC schedule via NANO).
  - Radiant's Part 70 factory-licence review target of 18 Dec 2026 is from the deck and was not re-verified.
- **DOE route vs NRC:** DOE authorises under its own Atomic Energy Act authority (NSDA, PDSA, DSA, readiness review, start-up): all five 2026 criticalities. The Army regulates its first Janus unit (EO 14299). Commercial sales off federal sites need the NRC (Eielson's contract does).

## Things the deck or live piece has wrong

- **Deck: diesel attribution.** The deck says $0.15–0.60/kWh is "INL/EXT-21-63214 citing UAA 2020". The INL report attributes that range to **NEI 2019a**. UAA 2020a's own figure is $0.35–0.60/kWh for small rural Alaska communities. Fix the citation.
- **Deck: Unity's date.** The deck says "Deployable Energy Unity 1 Jul". DOE says late on **30 Jun**, with the announcement on 1 Jul. Unity is also a **Launch Pad** reactor, not one of the Pilot Program selectees, so "five Pilot Program criticalities" is wrong. Four were Pilot Program reactors and one came through Launch Pad.
- **Deck: "all zero or near-zero power"** holds, but Valar reached 10 kWt; say "physics tests".
- **Deck: "Mature micro about 12 c".** INL gives $119/MWh in its table, "~$120" in the abstract and "~$150/MWh range" in the conclusion. Say "12 to 15 cents". Also note the reference plant is 7 MWe, not 20.
- **Deck: grid price.** Deck uses 2024 industrial 8.13 c; latest is **8.62 c (2025)**. Data centres may pay commercial (13.41 c); do not call 8 c "what a data centre pays" without a source.
- **Deck: "Pele delivery planned 2024... four years late".** BWXT's page gives no ship or power date beyond "2028". Keep "four years late" only if the 2022 release says delivery in 2024.
- **Deck: why the military buys.** The buyers' own texts say grid resilience and contested logistics; the diesel comparison is INL's and NEI's. Attribute it that way.
- **Deck: Eielson.** The deck does not size Eielson. If it is used, Oklo's filing says "at least 5 MWe".
- **Live piece (nuclear-for-ai):** nothing in this slice found wrong. It does not cover microreactors; its Meta, Constellation and Oklo facts match what I found.
