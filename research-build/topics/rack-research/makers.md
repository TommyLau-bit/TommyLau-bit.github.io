- 2030 targets from the deck: organic growth about 20% to 22% a year 2025 to 2030; adjusted operating margin "~27%+" in 2030 [primary].

### 1d. Why the margin rose, in Vertiv's own words [call]

- Q3 2026, CFO Craig Chamberlin: "The margin expansion was driven by strong sales volume increase, operating leverage, and continued productivity" [call, singjupost].
- Q2 2026, Chamberlin: "strong operational execution, continued productivity gains" and "favorable price cost execution, partially offset by tariff impacts" [call, Motley Fool].

## 5. What the live piece has wrong or out of date

1. **"Vertiv is the one maker to put a figure on what a megawatt holds."** Wrong. Schneider gave $1.2m to $3.3m per MW at its Capital Markets Day (11 Dec 2025, transcript p63) [primary], and said the most complex AI sites would not trend to the top of that range. That second sentence cuts against "more gear per megawatt" and should be conceded. The evidence fold's "I found no per-MW figure in ... Schneider Electric's own filings that I could open" also needs changing.
2. **The Vertiv call sentence** ("called 2027 the customer validation phase for its 800 volt range, with first revenue that year, and put deployment in 2028") merges two phases. Vertiv's own dates: rack and pod level under validation in 2026, deployment 2027; data hall level validated 2027, deployed 2028 (Q2 call, 29 Jul 2026) [call]. The sidecar, PowerDirect 5000, up to 900 kW, has "Commercial launch expected in early 2027" (deck, 20 May 2026) [primary]. Better to cite the deck than the garbled Q3 transcript.
3. **Vertiv's 800 VDC line has slipped against Nvidia's timetable**, which the claim `every-megawatt-got-harder` watches. Vertiv promised release "in the second half of 2026" (May and Oct 2025); its May 2026 deck says commercial launch early 2027. Nvidia still dates its power rack to H2 2026. The piece does not say this.
4. **Margin cause.** The piece says the Q3 2026 margin "fits operating leverage as well as richer gear". Vertiv's CFO attributes it to "strong sales volume increase, operating leverage, and continued productivity" [call]; content is not named. H1 2026 expansion was against tariff-hit 2025 quarters. This weakens the margin limb further.
5. **Backlog watch item is settled for now.** Vertiv stopped quarterly orders and backlog (Feb 2026), and on 28 Oct 2026 its chairman said it is "not planning to share orders data going forward" [call]. Last backlog $15.0bn at 31 Dec 2025. The Q3 "more than 100%" is a one-off headline.
6. **Services share is falling**, not just growing more slowly: 19.0% of sales in Q3 2025 to 16.7% in Q3 2026 [calc]. Worth one line beside the organic 18.1% against 63.6%.
7. **Acquisitions are now large and in new layers.** UIG ($1.45bn plus up to $1.15bn earnout, closed 26 to 27 Oct 2026, behind-the-meter microgrids) and PurgeRite (about $1.0bn). Per-MW "opportunity" growth partly reflects buying new scope, which the deck says ("~$13B increase in our TAM via organic and inorganic actions") [primary]. The piece should say so if it leans on the per-MW figure.
8. **"The building step described but not dated"** is true of Nvidia but out of date for makers. ABB dates its SST to H2 2027; Eaton ships 2 MW SST pilots in 2026 and expects 800 V DC project shipments from late 2027; Siemens and MR are industrialising a 36 kV SST (14 Aug 2026); Vertiv dates its data hall level to 2028. All fall after or near the claim window, which supports it, but the piece should name them.
9. **The sidecar now has a final standard.** Mt. Diablo 1.0.0 (8 Oct 2026, Microsoft, Meta, Google) specifies an AC-fed ±400 VDC power rack of 0.8 to 1.1 MW. That is direct support for "rack first, fed from AC", and a contrast with the SST specification at 0.3. Not in the piece.
10. **Exposure map, rack power.** Delta, Lite-On and Flex each launched an 800 VDC power rack at GTC (16 to 17 Mar 2026), not just shelves and converters; Delta's 660 kW in-row rack is said to be in mass shipment from Q3 2026 [press]. They compete with Vertiv, Schneider and Eaton for the same cabinet. "Makers of power racks" in the piece should include them.
11. **Eaton data centre figures** worth adding as evidence that power gear demand outruns megawatts: data centre sales +65%, orders +101% in Q3 2026 [call]; Fibrebond (prefabricated power rooms) from $375m to about $600m revenue [call].
12. Minor: the piece's chart caption dates Vertiv's GB200 132 kW design to 15 Oct 2024; Vertiv's deck now cites about 140 kW per rack today and 300 kW to 1 MW+ next [primary, 20 May 2026], a newer figure for the rack chart.
