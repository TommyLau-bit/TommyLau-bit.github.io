## What to learn (questions to answer from primary sources: BWXT 10-K and investor materials, DoD/DOE releases, NRC)
- What BWXT does: its segments, who pays it (US Navy naval reactors, DOE, commercial), how it makes money and how much.

- The fuel problem: high-assay low-enriched uranium (HALEU) supply, and Russia's role (links to nuclear-for-ai).
- The 2 to 3 numbers that matter (backlog, margins, fuel capacity, first-power dates).
- The dated claim and what would prove it wrong: for example, when the first microreactor makes power outside a government test site, and at what cost per MW.
