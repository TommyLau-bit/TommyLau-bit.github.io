## What the live piece has wrong or out of date

1. **Centrus output understated, and units mixed (chart 3 and the "One small line" paragraph).**
   - The piece says "900 kg in its second phase alone"; the caption says "earlier phases' output is not stated". Centrus's release of 1 Jul 2026 gives a **cumulative total of more than 1,900 kg** of HALEU UF6.
   - The chart also sets 0.9 t of **UF6** against 1 t of **uranium** (the task order) and 5 t of **uranium** (Oklo's EBR-II stock).
   - Fix: "about 1.9 t of HALEU gas, about 1.3 t of uranium, made in all to June 2026", against 1 tU owed by July 2032 and 5 tU behind Oklo.
2. **"Centrus's first new centrifuges in Ohio come online in 2029, and those are for ordinary fuel."** This is too strong.
   - The Q2 10-Q does tie the Oak Ridge centrifuges to LEU at Piketon.
   - But Centrus's 1 Jul 2026 release says the initial build-out includes **12 t/yr of HALEU capacity**, with first new capacity "by 2029".
