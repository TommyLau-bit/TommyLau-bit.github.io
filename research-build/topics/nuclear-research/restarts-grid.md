## 2. The NRC process for a restart

Before Palisades there was no rule for bringing a plant back once its owner had certified permanent shutdown and defuelling: 10 CFR 50.82(a)(2) says that once both certifications are docketed, the licence no longer authorises operation or fuel in the vessel. The path built for Palisades, and copied by Crane and Duane Arnold, is: an exemption from 50.82(a)(2) allowing a one-time rescission of the certifications, plus licence amendments restoring the operating licence/tech specs, emergency plan and security plan, plus an environmental assessment. The NRC said the exemption "is necessary... but is not sufficient"; all requests must be approved together. A restart inspection programme exists: Inspection Manual Chapter 2562 (ML24033A299).

## 3. The grid place

### 3a. What a CIR is in PJM, and how it is lost

| Point | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Definition | CIRs are "the rights to input generation as a Generation Capacity Resource into the Transmission System at the Point of Interconnection" (Tariff pt VII, §300(C)); awarded for MW accredited as deliverable | FERC order ER26-2028, P 5 |
| Why it matters | Capacity Resource status (and so the capacity market) requires PJM to find the output deliverable to load; an Energy Resource cannot sell capacity | same, P 3 |
| One-year rule | On Deactivation (or removal of Capacity Resource status) CIRs "terminate one year from the Deactivation Date" unless the holder has submitted a completed interconnection request claiming them (Tariff §328(C)(3)) | same, P 5 |
| Transfer | CIRs may be transferred to a new unit, but at a different point of interconnection PJM must study it and the transfer "may not be one-to-one" | same, PP 5, 16, 30 |
| Crane | Deactivated 20 Sep 2019; no new request within a year, so its CIRs lapsed (inference from the rule; Crane re-entered as a new request). PJM picked Crane as one of 51 Reliability Resource Initiative projects on 2 May 2025, putting it in Transition Cycle #2 | FERC order P 9; PJM Inside Lines 2 May 2025 |
| PJM study | Phase I System Impact Study results received 31 Oct 2025: numerous Contingent Facilities, including hundreds of miles of new 765 kV and 500 kV lines with in-service dates as late as Dec 2030; thermal violations on 10 facilities (seven 500 kV, three 230 kV). Until all are built Crane is only "interim deliverable", studied annually | FERC order PP 9-10, 22 |
| The 2030 date | FERC: "absent the waiver, Crane... may not be able to obtain full deliverability until at least December 31, 2030". This is Constellation's reading of PJM's Phase I study, accepted by FERC, not a PJM date as such | FERC order P 38 |
| Fallback cost | Withdrawing and resubmitting in Cycle #1 would have delayed an interconnection agreement "at least to 2028" | FERC order P 16 |

## What the live piece has wrong or out of date

1. "PJM... put full delivery through a fresh connection no sooner than 31 December 2030" (para at line 104, chart aria-label and caption). PJM did not state a date. PJM's Phase I System Impact Study (results 31 Oct 2025) listed Contingent Facilities with in-service dates as late as Dec 2030; Constellation argued, and FERC accepted, that Crane "may not be able to obtain full deliverability until at least December 31, 2030" (FERC order P 38). Reword: "PJM's first study tied Crane's full delivery to new lines due as late as December 2030."
2. "That buys about three years" and the chart title "Borrowed grid rights pull Crane's date forward by about three years". Overstated. The reactor's 2H 2027 date never depended on the plug; Crane could generate from 2027 as "interim deliverable" either way. FERC said the transfer could "potentially... enable Crane to be fully operational before December 31, 2030", and that PJM's studies control. Reword to: it may bring full delivery forward from the end of 2030, subject to PJM's studies.
3. "FERC... let 760 MW of grid rights move to Crane" is right on the number but the order is a waiver allowing the transfer request; the final MW credited at Crane's different connection point is for PJM's studies (transfer "may not be one-to-one"). Add the docket: ER26-2028-000, 195 FERC ¶ 61,162.
4. "Closing in 2019 cost it its grid place." Correct in effect, but say why: PJM's rule ends a retired plant's rights one year after deactivation unless a new request claims them.
5. Crane MW: the piece uses 835 MW (Constellation); FERC's order uses 859 MW. Keep 835 but be aware.
6. Duane Arnold grid place, table row "Not stated by NextEra": wrong. FERC waiver of 25 Aug 2025 (ER25-2989-000) kept up to 619 MW of MISO interconnection rights through the replacement process, with commercial operation by 31 Dec 2029. Duane Arnold is a "held plug" case too.
7. Duane Arnold financing missing: DOE loan up to US$1.9bn closed 8 Sep 2026; buyer Google (25-year PPA, 27 Oct 2025) plus CIPCO. The piece's "sources" cite NextEra 8 Sep 2026 but the body does not say it.
8. Palisades: the piece cites Holtec's "registration statement (8 September 2026)". Holtec filed to withdraw that registration on 25 Sep 2026 (EDGAR form RW). Facts stand, but describe it as "Holtec's withdrawn IPO filing" or cite the NRC/FERC documents instead. The same filing said restart "in 2026"; that has slipped.
9. Palisades new facts to add: NRC emergency amendment for the tilted assembly approved 13 Oct 2026 (ML26282A040); NRC inspection report 13 Oct 2026 found a "chilled work environment" (ML26286A257); switchyard reconnected 11 Sep 2026.
10. Crane NRC: "NRC approvals expected early 2027" is consistent with the NRC's target of 4 Mar 2027 (schedule letter ML25345A027). Add final EA/FONSI 18 Sep 2026 and Amendment 307 (new fuel, 1 Jul 2026). Constellation's own phrase is "second half of 2027".
11. Eddystone: "running only under federal emergency orders" is right; the current order (202-26-40) runs to 20 Nov 2026, six orders in all since 30 May 2025. If not renewed the units may retire; the CIRs are already being moved by the waiver.
12. Palisades MISO date and FERC date (1 Mar 2027; 28 Feb 2025, ER25-1083) check out. The rule is 36 cumulative months of suspension in five years.
13. Watch: Constellation's Q3 2026 results (expected about 9 Nov 2026, press) may update Crane's date.
