# the-queue-not-the-chip: Johari Hansard update (2 Nov 2026)

Origin: sweep 2026-11, update 2 (held until the 28 Oct Hansard was read). Tommy, 2 Nov: "take on the initiative and fix them". The Hansard does NOT support the press line that data centre investors pay for transmission upgrades and for allocated capacity whether used or not (searched the whole day's record). It does carry new primary facts, which this update uses instead.

Source: Dewan Rakyat Hansard, 28 October 2026, https://www.parlimen.gov.my/files/hindex/pdf/DR-28102026.pdf . Minister of Investment, Trade and Industry, Datuk Seri Johari bin Abdul Ghani, speaking from 3.22pm (printed p. 96):
- p. 97: 117 data centres approved: 55 operating, 19 under construction, 16 not started, 27 partly started; "yang 27 ini power yang dia akan dapat adalah melalui off grid".
- p. 98: data centre power is separated from the 27 or 28 industries TNB serves; no subsidy; follows world market prices.
- p. 99: the DCTF (MITI, Digital, PETRA) ensures data centres do not hurt other industry: reserve margin "kena properly spare dan kita cuma bagi data center ini kalau ada excess. Kalau tak ada excess, kita buat off-grid"; off-grid can be "any power", e.g. solar with batteries.
- p. 100: off-grid approval from the PETRA ministry, through ST (the Energy Commission).
- p. 101: for any approval beyond the 117, TNB is the first input; moratorium: "At this moment tak ada perancangan, tapi perancangan kita moratorium cara kita proses".
- p. 104: "dah masuk 24 application data centre, kita tak ada bagi apa-apa jawapan sebab kita nak tahu 117 ini settle".
- The Deputy Prime Minister's solar answer in the existing paragraph was at question time, about 10.10 to 10.20am (pp. 6 to 9), hence "Later that day".

Changes:
1. Summary, last sentence: own power in Malaysia is allowed only once the grid's spare margin is gone, and only with a permit (was: Malaysia steers data centres to solar).
2. Plain-English top, paragraph on own power: same correction.
3. "Malaysia closes the route" became "narrows"; "Gas stays with the grid" became "The grid's own gas is spoken for" (Johari floated PETRONAS gas at market price for separated data centre power, p. 98).
4. New paragraph, anchor `malaysia-grants-only-the-spare-margin`: grid power only from spare margin; off-grid otherwise, by permit; 27 of 117 approved off-grid; no moratorium plan "at this moment"; 24 applications unanswered.
5. "So the American escape does not travel" became "travels only halfway": in Malaysia own power is the route left once the spare margin runs out, and the permit still decides. This says openly where the new evidence cuts against the earlier wording.
