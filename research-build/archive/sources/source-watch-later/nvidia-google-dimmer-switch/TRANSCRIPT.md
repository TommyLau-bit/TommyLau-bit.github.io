The grid is not governed by an annual electricity allowance It must balance supply and demand continuously. And the equipment carrying that electricity has physical limits. Here's a simplified example. Suppose a local system can support 100 units of demand. during a quiet period, existing customers use 60. a new data center uses 30. The total is 90. There's room. But if the data center temporarily reduce its demand from 30 to 15, the total returns to 100. This does not make the electricity use disappear. It changes how much power the facility needs during the difficult period. but what computing work can possibly wait? From outside, a data center looks like one enormous machine. inside, its computers are doing very different jobs. Imagine three examples. A live AI answer is needed now. That service must remain protected. A collection of documents must be processed by tomorrow morning. It can progress more slowly for a while if it still meets the deadline. An AI training job may tolerate a temporary slowdown, but only if its customer has agreed. The opportunity is not switch off the building. It is to treat these jobs according to their real timing requirements. Computers already have controls that can limit chip power, change how many processors a job receives, or pause and resume suitable work. The difficult part is coordinating those controls without breaking a customer promise. This is what Emerald AI is trying to do Emerald makes software called Conductor. It connects what the electricity system needs with what each computing job is allowed to do. A reduction request arrives. Lower power by a specified amount. Begin at a specified time and continue for a specified duration. Conductor also received the rules for the computing jobs. Protect the live answer. Finish the documents by morning. Adjust the training job only within the customer's permission. Now it needs a plan. Emerald uses a simulator to evaluate these trade-offs before applying a response. In our example, the selected plan protects the live answer, slow the document job without missing its deadline, and temporarily adjust the training job. Conductor coordinates those changes with the computing systems. The meter falls But the system also tracks the job performance. A lower power rating only counts as a successful response if the computing commitments were respected. That is the technology behind our dial. In Phoenix, Emerald and its partners tested representative AI workloads on a cluster of 256 NVIDIA GPUs inside an Oracle data center.
