THE RULES (CALLS-SPEC.md "The book" is the master copy; follow it if it differs)
- Book US$100,000 from 6 Nov 2026. Sizes by conviction: HIGH 12 per cent, MEDIUM 7, LOW 4, of current equity; max 15 per cent in one name; at most 10 open positions and 80 per cent invested. Halve the size if the company reports results within the next 10 trading days (results can gap through a stop).
- ENTRY (long; mirror for short): all of (1) the note's base value (src/data/notes.ts BASE_VALUE; a note missing there is not traded until its value is added, so flag it) is at least 15 per cent above the close for a long, or at least 25 per cent below for a short; (2) a low has held: the lowest close of the last 20 sessions is at least 3 sessions old; (3) today's close is above the highest close of the previous 5 sessions; (4) stop = 1 per cent under that 20-session low; target = the lower of the base value and the highest close of the last 60 sessions; reward to risk (target minus close) over (close minus stop) is at least 2. Fill at that close. Skip a name on the "Not buying" list unless its reason has gone (say so in the log). Business tests must not have broken (the note's wrongIf non-price legs).
- EXIT: the first close at or through the target ("Target hit") or through the stop ("Stopped out") closes the position at that close. Also close if a non-price wrongIf leg of the note triggers ("Thesis broken"). Every exit gets a post-mortem of two or three sentences, scored on process, not outcome: what happened, whether the rules were followed, the lesson.
- Stops never move against the position. Once a long is up by its full risk (1R), move its stop to the entry price; log it.
- Drawdown: if equity is 10 per cent or more below its peak, open no new positions until a full review is written in the log (Radar rule).
- Sundays: weekly review only, no new positions: three to five sentences on what worked, what did not, and net exposure by theme.

STEPS
1. Price every name and the FX pairs at the latest completed close. Record the date per market.
2. Exits first: for each open position test every close since its last mark (not just today) against stop and target; close at the first one hit. Then non-price wrongIf checks against any company release or filing since the last run (dated, primary source).
3. Marks: update each open position's last, lastDate, value (US$), result (signed fraction, a correct short is positive) and pnlUsd; recompute cash and equity (cash plus marked values); append {date, equity} to equityCurve (one per run date).
4. Entries (not Sundays, not in a drawdown pause): run the entry test on every name; open the best by reward to risk first, within the size and count limits. A new position records slug, company, ticker, currency, direction, conviction, entryDate, entryPrice, fxAtEntry, shares, sizeUsd, stop, target, thesis (one line), wrongIf (from the note), why (two or three sentences: what is priced in, what is mispriced, the risk), management (array of dated lines). Also give it `what` (one plain line on what the company does).
5. Watchlist: rebuild `watch` for every eligible name not held: close, signal (the 5-session closing high), stop, target, targetFrom, rr, status ("Waiting: still making new lows" / "Waiting: low holding, needs a close above the signal" / "Signal reached but reward to risk below 2" / "Results due <date>, half size"), what (one plain line), why (one sentence, updated if news changed it). Shorts mirror the long rule: signal = the 5-session closing low, stop = 1 per cent above the 20-session closing high (`stopFrom` says where, e.g. "just above the October high"), target = the higher of the base value and the 60-session closing low. When no signal can be set (for example the price already sits on its 60-day low, so there is no room to a target), set signal and rr to null but keep stop and target, and write `plan`: two or three plain sentences on why there is no trade yet and what would arm it. Never leave a card empty. Re-check `avoid` reasons; move a name to watch if its reason has gone.
6. exposure: two sentences on cash and net exposure by theme. log: append one entry {date, text} of at most three sentences: equity and day P&L, every entry and exit with price, anything notable. Set asOf (today) and pricesAt (latest close date used).
