Here is a claim I am willing to be wrong about in public. A chipmaker planning new AI capacity has to book one machine a year or two ahead, the way a data centre books its grid connection.

That machine is ASML's EUV scanner. ASML, in Veldhoven in the Netherlands, builds the machines that print the finest layers of a leading-edge chip, and nobody else makes them.

It expects to ship every standard one it can build this year. Next year is already close to fully ordered.

ASML's EUV machines are sold out and booked a year or two ahead, and their number grows only as fast as ZEISS and ASML's cleanrooms allow.

<details class="analogy">
<summary>Explain it like I don't work in finance</summary>
<p>Think of a new bakery. A builder can fit out the shop in months. But the special oven comes from one workshop in the whole country, and next year's ovens are already promised.</p>
<p>The workshop can make a few more each year, but only as fast as its glassmaker makes oven doors. So a baker opening in two years orders the oven today. ASML is that workshop.</p>
</details>

## Why only ASML builds them

An EUV scanner is a lithography machine, which prints a chip's circuit pattern onto a silicon wafer with extreme ultraviolet light, a very short wavelength that draws the finest features.

That light is absorbed by almost everything, including air and glass, so the light path sits in a high vacuum and is steered by mirrors. To make it, a laser from Trumpf fires at tiny drops of tin up to 50,000 times a second.

Supply cannot simply catch up. ASML's annual report says the number of machines it can make "is limited by the production capacity" of Carl Zeiss SMT. ZEISS is its only supplier of the critical optics, made only at two sites in Germany. ASML owns about a quarter of that business and lends it money to expand. A company pays for a supplier's factories only when that supplier sets its own limit.

## How far ahead the machines are booked

In July ASML put this year's capacity for standard EUV machines at around 65, and expects to ship all of them. Its chief financial officer said it was "close to being fully covered with orders" for 2027.

ASML plans to raise capacity to around 85 next year. Its chief executive said the increase comes from existing buildings, without new cleanrooms, the dust-free halls where the machines are assembled. A chipmaker that decides late joins the back of a queue that is already set.

## What would prove me wrong

The booking half fails if ASML says it has EUV capacity beyond what customers have ordered, or ships more than 10 per cent short of its stated capacity and blames demand. It also fails if, by January 2027, ASML no longer calls 2027 close to fully covered.

The growth half fails if ASML raises standard EUV capacity by more than 40 per cent in one year, or announces new EUV cleanrooms or a second optics source.
